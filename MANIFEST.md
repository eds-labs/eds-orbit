# Framework Manifest

Das Codex Project Framework integriert drei Komponenten:

- Agentic Scaffold: Projektregeln, Rollen, Workflows, App-Profile, Templates und Basis-Quality-Gates
- Stack Presets: Stack-Routing, technologiebezogene Presets und passende App-Strukturen
- Operational Governance: Risiko, Environments, Security, Datenbank-Lifecycle, CI/CD, Deployment, Observability, Compliance und Freigaben
